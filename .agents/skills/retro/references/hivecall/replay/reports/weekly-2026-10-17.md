## Decision

- **This week:** no PR; leave the skill unchanged. The proposal did not produce a qualifying, evaluable win. The B3 result is underpowered, not a measured regression.
- **Unlock:** gather enough additional archived runs to reach at least 10 held-out B3 moments, then rerun the same proposal and replay.
- **Cause class:** `artifact_gap`. The proposal identifies a missing turn-end liveness check in the owning skill; the gate judged this narrow change worth evaluating. The replay has not shown whether it improves outcomes. [Proposal replay](eval-pia-b3-vs-head.md)
- **Generalization check:** the check could apply whenever a worker may return without a result while items remain open; the current held-out B3 sample is too small to confirm its benefit. [Proposal replay](eval-pia-b3-vs-head.md)
- **Retro verdict:** `NO_CHANGE` for this week. This means no change is approved without a replay win; it does not mean the observed failure or proposal was disproved.
